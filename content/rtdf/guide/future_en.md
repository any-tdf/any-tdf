## Current Status

RTDF 0.0.1 is the initial stable release. The existing component system is available for React, with shared themes and locales, framework-native APIs, Demos, and bilingual documentation.

## Ongoing Work

- Keep shared behavior aligned with STDF and VTDF, while preserving React events, hooks, and children.
- Improve accessibility, mobile interaction, SSR, and browser regression coverage.
- Expand component examples, translations, and theming documentation.
- Evaluate new components and business patterns from community feedback.
